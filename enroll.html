How enrollment works

Enrollment is rolling, based on open spaces in your child's class and age group. Siblings of current students get priority; all other spots are filled first come, first served.

  1. Tour with our director. In person or virtual. Please bring official ID, and bring your child if you can.
  2. Classroom visit. Your child is invited to spend an hour in class so the move to preschool feels easy.
  3. Application and fees. Submit the required Florida DCF paperwork, your child's immunization and health forms from the pediatrician, and enrollment fees within 72 hours to hold your space.
  4. Choose a start date. The director helps plan the first week, which starts with at least one half-day visit.
