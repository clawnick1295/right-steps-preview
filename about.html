About Right Steps Academy & Our Teachers

Right Steps Academy is a licensed early learning school in Fort Myers, Florida, where children from 12 months through VPK learn through play in a warm, safe setting.

Our mission

To create an interactive, warm and loving environment that helps each child develop and learn, using best-practice teaching and individualized strategies suited to every child in our program.

Our vision

To pursue the best quality in early childhood education through best practices, stimulating learning materials, an environment that adapts to children's needs, and highly trained teachers.

Our philosophy

Every child's physical, mental, social and emotional well-being deserves the best possible consideration, at all times and in every situation.

Licensing & accreditation

Right Steps Academy is a licensed child care facility, regulated by the Florida Department of Children and Families (license #C20LE0270). Families can look up any Florida provider's license and inspection reports on the state's child care provider search.

APPLE accredited. We hold APPLE Accreditation (Accredited Professional Preschool Learning Environment) from the Florida Association for Child Care Management. APPLE is a nationally recognized early learning accreditation built on self-evaluation and verified quality standards.

Gold Seal Quality Care. Because we are accredited by a state-approved agency, Right Steps Academy is a Florida Gold Seal Quality Care provider. Gold Seal is Florida's recognition for child care programs that go above minimum licensing standards. It is administered by the Florida Department of Education's Division of Early Learning.
