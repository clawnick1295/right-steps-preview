Right Steps Academy FAQ: Fort Myers Preschool & Daycare

Straight answers about our hours, ages, tuition, meals, VPK, licensing and enrollment. Still have a question? Call 239-318-1102.

Where is Right Steps Academy located?

Right Steps Academy is at 12800 Kenwood Ln, Fort Myers, FL 33907, in Lee County. We serve families across Fort Myers, including nearby South Fort Myers, Cypress Lake and Iona. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: neighborhoods/areas served list]

What are Right Steps Academy's hours?

We are open Monday through Friday from 7:00am to 6:00pm. We are closed on weekends and holidays.

What ages does Right Steps Academy serve?

We enroll children from 12 months old through VPK (children who are 4 years old by September 1). Our classrooms are Toddlers (12–24 months), Two's (24–36 months), Three's (36–48 months) and VPK. Kindergarten is coming soon.

How much does daycare cost at Right Steps Academy in Fort Myers?

Tuition for Toddlers, Two's and Three's is $1,100 per 4-week period. VPK Wraparound care for 4- and 5-year-olds is $800 per 4-week period. Tuition is billed in 4-week periods, not calendar months, and it includes breakfast, lunch and afternoon snack. There are no hidden fees. Are meals included in tuition?

Yes. Every child gets breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack each school day, all included in tuition. Meals are catered fresh and planned to meet early childhood dietary guidelines.

Does Right Steps Academy offer VPK in Fort Myers?

Yes. Our VPK classroom is for children who turn 4 by September 1. Florida's Voluntary Prekindergarten (VPK) program is free for eligible 4-year-olds, and our VPK Wraparound care ($800 per 4 weeks) covers the rest of the 7:00am–6:00pm day, with breakfast, lunch and afternoon snack included.

Is Right Steps Academy licensed and accredited?

Yes. Right Steps Academy is a licensed child care facility in Florida (DCF license #C20LE0270), a Florida Gold Seal Quality Care provider, and APPLE accredited (Accredited Professional Preschool Learning Environment, from the Florida Association for Child Care Management). Gold Seal is Florida's designation for child care programs accredited by a state-approved accrediting agency.

What curriculum does Right Steps Academy use?

We use an inquiry-based, play-based curriculum with a STEAM approach (science, technology, engineering, arts and math) and project-based learning. Teachers work in small groups so each child learns at their own level, and our character development program teaches eight traits aligned with Lee County Schools.

How do you track my child's development?

We use the Ages and Stages Questionnaire (ASQ), a parent-completed developmental screening, and then meet with families to review results. VPK students also take Florida's state-required FAST Star Early Literacy assessment three times a year, and parents receive a report after each one. How do you keep children safe?

Our campus has keypad entry and access control, security cameras, background-checked staff, and a written emergency plan with regular fire drills and first aid training. Classrooms are cleaned and sanitized on a continuous schedule.

How will I stay updated about my child's day?

Families get a parent app with daily updates, photos and important notices, and you see your child's teacher at every drop-off and pick-up. Parents are also welcome at school events and classroom activities throughout the year.

Who are the director and teachers at Right Steps Academy?

Our director, Cindy Goldberg, brings over 30 years of early childhood experience. All of our teachers are certified early childhood professionals who are background-checked. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: teacher credentials to name, e.g. DCF 45-hour / CDA]

How do I enroll or schedule a tour?

Call 239-318-1102 or request a tour online. Enrollment is rolling, based on open spaces in your child's age group. Siblings of current students get priority; all other spots are first come, first served. The steps are: tour, classroom visit, application and fees, then choosing a start date.

Does Right Steps Academy offer kindergarten?

Kindergarten is coming soon. Call us to be added to the interest list and to hear when enrollment opens.
