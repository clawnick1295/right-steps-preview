VPK in Fort Myers, FL with Full-Day Wraparound Care

Right Steps Academy offers Florida's free Voluntary Prekindergarten (VPK) program for children who are 4 years old by September 1, plus optional wraparound care so your child can stay from 7:00am to 6:00pm. Wraparound is $800 per 4-week period, with breakfast, lunch and afternoon snack included.

Kindergarten readiness you can see

VPK students take Florida's state-required FAST Star Early Literacy assessment three times a year. It measures letter recognition, phonics, vocabulary and early math, and parents receive a detailed report after each testing period.

We also use the Ages and Stages Questionnaire (ASQ), a parent-completed developmental screening covering communication, motor skills, problem solving and personal-social skills. After each screening, families meet with the teacher or director to celebrate progress and plan next steps.

Our inquiry-based STEAM curriculum and character development program help children arrive at kindergarten curious, confident and kind.
