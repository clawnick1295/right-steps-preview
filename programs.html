Ages 12–24 months

Toddlers

Our toddler classroom is designed around how one-year-olds learn: by moving, touching and copying the people they love. The room has well-defined spaces that support relationships and learning:

  • A quiet book corner with soft pillows, where toddlers can calm down and begin building pre-reading skills
  • Active play with climbers, small steps and large balls for big-muscle development
  • Block and dramatic play areas with a play kitchen, dolls and mirrors for early language and social skills
  • An art area with easels, plus a sand and water sensory table
  • Child-size sinks and restroom to begin potty training
  • A shaded outdoor area next to the classroom with riding toys, small climbers, a playhouse and a covered sand area

A sample toddler day

  • 7:00–8:30am Arrival, handwashing, diapers/potty, breakfast
  • 8:30–9:30am Sensory & discovery, story/circle time, fine motor, music & movement
  • 9:30–10:30am Centers: art, science, reading, blocks, fine motor
  • 10:30–11:30am Outdoor play
  • 11:30am–12:30pm Math & logic games
  • 12:30–1:30pm Handwashing, lunch, get ready for nap
  • 1:30–3:30pm Nap / quiet time
  • 3:30–4:00pm Handwashing, afternoon snack
  • 4:00–4:30pm Gross motor & outdoor time
  • 4:30–5:30pm Reading, sensory activities and centers
  • 5:30–6:00pm Departure

Schedules are subject to change.

Ages 24–36 months

Two's

Two-year-olds are ready for more independence and more words. Our Two's classroom is organized into learning centers so children can choose, explore and practice new skills:

  • Reading corner and library for circle time, pre-reading and early writing
  • Math and manipulatives such as puzzles and building sets for counting, sorting and ordering
  • Science lab for exploring colors, plants, bugs, magnets and more
  • Dramatic play and housekeeping for language and communication
  • Blocks, art and music for creativity and fine motor skills
  • A shaded playground next to the classroom for daily outdoor play
Ages 36–48 months

Three's

In the Three's classroom, children settle into preschool routines and build the early literacy, math and social skills they'll use in VPK. Learning happens in small groups and hands-on centers, including reading, math and manipulatives, science, dramatic play, blocks, and art and music. The day includes morning and afternoon outdoor time on a shaded playground.

Teachers plan lessons around each child's progress, so children who are ready for a challenge get one and children who need more time get it.

How children learn at Right Steps Academy

Play-based & inquiry-based

“Playing is learning.” Children explore, ask questions and learn by doing, through activities matched to their age and stage.

STEAM & project-based

Science, technology, engineering, arts and math are woven into in-depth projects on real-world topics.

Small groups

Teachers work with small groups so every child gets one-on-one time and learns at their own level.

Character development

Eight traits aligned with Lee County Schools: citizenship, cooperation, honesty, kindness, respect, responsibility, self-control and tolerance.

Daily enrichment

Music, movement, literacy and art every day, plus weekly take-home art projects.
